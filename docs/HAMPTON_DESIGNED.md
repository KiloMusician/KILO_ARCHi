# Hampton Designed

**Status: approved product policy for the ARCHi Alpha direction.** The creator seal, distinctive creator editions and balanced signature abilities are approved design goals. Issuance, signing, creator authentication, verified editions, ownership transfers and a marketplace are future capabilities. This document does not certify an Alpha character or announce a released service.

## Code permission and creator endorsement

ARCHi's code license is MIT. Its permission to use, copy, modify, distribute and otherwise work with the licensed code is governed by `LICENSE`. This product policy adds no conditions to those permissions and does not require an official seal to develop a fork or companion.

**Hampton Designed** is intended to communicate a specific creator endorsement: Patrick Hampton has authored or expressly approved the identified design and the recorded version of its appearance and game abilities. A source fork, copied UID or displayed badge alone is not evidence of that endorsement. Verification must be tied to an identifiable artifact and accountable issuer before the app presents it as verified.

This policy does not assert patent or trademark rights. It does not grant rights to third-party artwork, models or tools; their applicable notices remain separate from the code license.

## What each record means

| Record | Meaning | Alpha status |
|---|---|---|
| Individual UID / Journey origin | Continuity for one local character history and repeatable presentation. | Existing local identifiers and origin digest; not proof of global uniqueness or authenticity. |
| Creator provenance | Evidence that an identified issuer authored or endorsed a particular artifact and version. | Future verified record and issuer-authentication mechanism. |
| Edition record | An issuer's declared collection, edition size and individual issue number. | Future capability; no enforced scarcity or issuance registry. |
| Ownership or entitlement | A separately defined, verifiable right associated with a character or edition. | Future capability; no authenticated owner or transfer service. |
| Signature ability | A named ability with rules, costs and behavior in a specific battle ruleset. | Existing role abilities belong to the current engine; Hampton Designed edition abilities are not yet implemented or balance-qualified. |
| Value | Distinct artistic, personal, gameplay and possible market assessments. | No price oracle, investment value, resale guarantee or sale validation. |

A UID cannot authenticate itself. A hash can identify content and detect changes when compared with a trusted reference; it does not establish who created the content or whether a transfer is authorized. A copied local save does not become a newly issued official edition. Edition limits and ownership claims need their own trusted records and verification.

## Distinctive appearance and continuous identity

A Hampton Designed companion may have a recognizable creator-authored appearance, palette, materials, markings and expressive details. The planned seal should identify the approved design version so later changes can be reviewed and attributed.

Appearance remains separate from authority and memories. A new body must not silently change permissions, replace the individual's history or turn an inferred user preference into a confirmed fact.

The current Alpha gives supported Companion/Pearl and Lumen appearances subtle, repeatable individual variation derived from Journey origin. Larger forms remain explicit choices, with independent history and explicit Evolution Save/Load. These implemented features do not demonstrate automatic learned biological growth, creator authentication or collectible scarcity.

## Balanced signature abilities

Creator editions may express different play styles through signature abilities. They must use the same ruleset's power budget and resource limits as comparable participants. Distinctiveness can come from timing, targeting, support effects, combinations or tactical tradeoffs.

Before an ability is accepted into a shared ruleset:

1. Specify its legal targets, resource cost, timing, limits and interaction with other abilities.
2. State its tradeoff and the opponent's available counterplay. Where applicable, specify cooldowns or use limits.
3. Check deterministic resolution, replay and legality in the existing battle engine.
4. Compare representative matchups under matched starting resources and record the tested ruleset version. Equal nominal costs alone do not prove balance.
5. Review balance changes as versioned rules changes, preserving historical encounter interpretation.

Payment, rarity, ownership status and the creator seal must not bypass these constraints or grant an automatic win. A paid appearance must not silently change battle statistics. The Alpha's existing battle engine is a starting point for this qualification; it is not evidence that future creator abilities have passed it.

## Private assistance stays private

Private assistant memories are excluded from transferable character records. This includes kept lessons, questions, conversations, shared documents, personal preferences, private Journey details, spatial history and credentials. A character transfer must not carry the previous person's assistant permissions or connected accounts.

A future transfer format should contain only a defined set of eligible public character facts, such as the public design identifier, verified issuer reference, edition record and ruleset ability identifiers. Explicitly shared public achievements would need a separate reviewable export choice. Keep the private working companion and its owner-controlled local records separate from that public record.

No transfer implementation is provided by this Alpha. This is the boundary a later implementation must satisfy, not a claim that current Journey exports are suitable for a marketplace.

## Acceptance before a verified seal or transfer ships

- Define the issuer, endorsement scope, design version and public verification record; verify these before displaying a verified seal.
- Test altered artifacts, forged issuers, copied badges and duplicate or revoked records, with clear unverified and unavailable states.
- Define edition issuance and transfer semantics independently of the local UID, including recovery and conflicting claims.
- Prove that private assistant data and account access are absent from public and transferable records.
- Qualify each signature ability within a versioned common battle ruleset using documented matchups and counterplay.
- Review applicable asset permissions and attribution separately from the MIT code license.

Until those checks exist and pass, the app must describe creator provenance, editions and ownership as unverified or unavailable. Publication of the code or this policy does not complete them.
